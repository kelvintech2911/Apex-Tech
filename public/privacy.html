Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Apex Tech ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of all individuals who interact with our services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, use our services, or communicate with us.

By using our website or engaging our services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and any details you provide through our contact form or consultation requests.
  • Case Information: Details related to your investigation requirements, including device types, incident descriptions, and supporting documentation you choose to share.
  • Website Usage Data: Browser type, IP address, pages visited, and interaction patterns collected through standard web analytics.
  • Referral & Campaign Data: Where applicable, information about how you reached our site, such as the referring website and any advertising campaign identifiers included in the link you followed. This is recorded with your enquiry so we understand which of our campaigns it came from.
  • Communication Records: Records of correspondence between you and our team via email, phone, WhatsApp, or Telegram.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and consultation requests.
  • Provide digital investigation and forensic services as agreed.
  • Communicate updates about your case or our services.
  • Improve our website and service delivery.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Data Protection & Security

We take the security of your data seriously. All personal and case-related information is handled with strict confidentiality. Our security measures include:

  • Encrypted communications for sensitive data transmission.
  • Restricted access — only authorized personnel handle your information.
  • Secure storage systems with appropriate access controls.
  • Regular review and updating of our security practices.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent: When you have given explicit permission.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.
  • Service delivery: With trusted partners directly involved in delivering our services, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Service providers: With providers who operate parts of this website on our behalf. Enquiries submitted through our forms are processed and delivered to us by Web3Forms, and our website analytics and advertising measurement are provided by the platforms described in section 8. These providers process the data only to provide their service to us.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including meeting legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Case-related data is retained in accordance with applicable professional and legal standards.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at apextechforensics@gmail.com.

8. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: Cookies and browser storage required for the site to function, including remembering how you arrived at the site during your visit.
  • Analytics: We use website analytics to understand how visitors find and use our site, such as which pages are viewed and how long visitors stay.
  • Advertising and measurement: We advertise on third-party platforms, including Meta (Facebook and Instagram). These platforms place cookies and receive information about your visit so that we can measure the performance of our advertising, understand which campaigns lead to enquiries, and show relevant ads to people who have visited our site.

Where we run advertising, information such as your IP address, browser and device details, the pages you view, and whether you submit an enquiry may be shared with the advertising platform. This is used for measurement and audience purposes and is subject to that platform’s own privacy policy. You can review Meta’s at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can opt out of interest-based advertising through your Meta ad preferences or through your device’s advertising settings. Blocking non-essential cookies will not prevent you from using this site or contacting us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use these technologies to access anything on your device beyond the standard information your browser provides.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
